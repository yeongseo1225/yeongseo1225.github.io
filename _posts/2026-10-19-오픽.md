### **1. 여가 생활 및 취미 (Pattern 1 ~ 24)**

- **Pattern 1:** I really enjoy ~ in my free time. _(자유 시간에 ~하는 것을 정말 즐깁니다.)_
    1. I really enjoy listening to music in my free time.
        
    2. I really enjoy going to cafes in my free time.
        
    3. I really enjoy working out at the gym in my free time.
        
- **Pattern 2:** One of my favorite activities is ~. _(내가 가장 좋아하는 활동 중 하나는 ~이다.)_
    
    1. One of my favorite activities is watching movies.
        
    2. One of my favorite activities is going hiking.
        
    3. One of my favorite activities is walking in the park.
        
- **Pattern 3:** I usually ~ when I have free time. _(나는 보통 여유 시간이 생기면 ~한다.)_
    
    1. I usually listen to music when I have free time.
        
    2. I usually go to a cafe when I have free time.
        
    3. I usually stay home and relax when I have free time.
        
- **Pattern 4:** I often ~ on weekends. _(나는 주말에 종종 ~한다.)_
    
    1. I often travel domestically on weekends.
        
    2. I often visit cafes on weekends.
        
    3. I often go hiking on weekends.
        
- **Pattern 5:** Sometimes I ~ with my friends. _(나는 가끔 친구들과 ~한다.)_
    
    1. Sometimes I watch movies with my friends.
        
    2. Sometimes I go to cafes with my friends.
        
    3. Sometimes I travel with my friends.
        
- **Pattern 6:** Most of the time I ~ alone. _(대부분의 경우 나는 혼자 ~한다.)_
    
    1. Most of the time I listen to music alone.
        
    2. Most of the time I watch movies alone.
        
    3. Most of the time I walk in the park alone.
        
- **Pattern 7:** I especially like ~ because it helps me relax. _(나는 ~를 특히 좋아하는데, 긴장을 푸는 데 도움이 되기 때문이다.)_
    
    1. I especially like listening to music because it helps me relax.
        
    2. I especially like going hiking because it helps me relax.
        
    3. I especially like going to cafes because it helps me relax.
        
- **Pattern 8:** It's a great way to ~. _(~하기에 아주 좋은 방법이다.)_
    
    1. It's a great way to stay healthy.
        
    2. It's a great way to relieve stress.
        
    3. It's a great way to relax.
        
- **Pattern 9:** It makes me feel ~. _(그것은 나에게 ~한 기분이 들게 한다.)_
    
    1. It makes me feel relaxed.
        
    2. It makes me feel energetic.
        
    3. It makes me feel refreshed.
        
- **Pattern 10:** There is a great place near my house where I go to ~. _(내가 ~하러 가는 우리 집 근처의 멋진 장소가 있다.)_
    
    1. There is a great place near my house where I go to a cafe.
        
    2. There is a great place near my house where I walk in the park.
        
    3. There is a great place near my house where I work out.
        
- **Pattern 11:** The ~ is located not far from where I live. _(~는 내가 사는 곳에서 멀지 않은 곳에 위치해 있다.)_
    
    1. The cafe is located not far from where I live.
        
    2. The park is located not far from where I live.
        
    3. The gym is located not far from where I live.
        
- **Pattern 12:** I remember one memorable experience when I ~. _(내가 ~했을 때의 기억에 남는 경험이 하나 기억난다.)_
    
    1. I remember one memorable experience when I traveled to Busan.
        
    2. I remember one memorable experience when I went hiking.
        
    3. I remember one memorable experience when I visited a unique cafe.
        
- **Pattern 13:** Something interesting happened when I ~. _(내가 ~했을 때 재미있는 일이 일어났다.)_
    
    1. Something interesting happened when I traveled to Jeju Island.
        
    2. Something interesting happened when I went hiking in the mountains.
        
    3. Something interesting happened when I visited a unique cafe.
        
- **Pattern 14:** It was one of the most memorable moments when I ~. _(~했을 때는 가장 기억에 남는 순간들 중 하나였다.)_
    
    1. It was one of the most memorable moments when I traveled with my friends.
        
    2. It was one of the most memorable moments when I reached the top of the mountain.
        
    3. It was one of the most memorable moments when I watched a great movie.
        
- **Pattern 15:** However, I once had a problem when I ~. _(하지만 나는 ~했을 때 한 번 문제가 생긴 적이 있었다.)_
    
    1. However, I once had a problem when I traveled abroad.
        
    2. However, I once had a problem when I ordered coffee at a cafe.
        
    3. However, I once had a problem when I went hiking.
        
- **Pattern 16:** Something unexpected happened when I ~. _(내가 ~했을 때 예상치 못한 일이 일어났다.)_
    
    1. Something unexpected happened when I traveled to Busan.
        
    2. Something unexpected happened when I watched a movie at the theater.
        
    3. Something unexpected happened when I visited a cafe.
        
- **Pattern 17:** Compared to the past, people ~ much more these days. _(과거에 비해 요즘 사람들은 훨씬 더 ~한다.)_
    
    1. Compared to the past, people listen to music online much more these days.
        
    2. Compared to the past, people go to cafes much more these days.
        
    3. Compared to the past, people travel abroad much more these days.
        
- **Pattern 18:** These days more people enjoy ~. _(요즘에는 더 많은 사람들이 ~를 즐긴다.)_
    
    1. These days more people enjoy going hiking.
        
    2. These days more people enjoy working out.
        
    3. These days more people enjoy going to cafes.
        
- **Pattern 19:** That's why I enjoy ~ so much. _(그것이 내가 ~를 그토록 즐기는 이유이다.)_
    
    1. That's why I enjoy listening to music so much.
        
    2. That's why I enjoy hiking so much.
        
    3. That's why I enjoy traveling so much.
        
- **Pattern 20:** That's why ~ has become an important part of my life. _(그것이 ~가 내 삶의 중요한 부분이 된 이유이다.)_
    
    1. That's why working out has become an important part of my life.
        
    2. That's why listening to music has become an important part of my life.
        
    3. That's why going to cafes has become an important part of my life.
        
- **Pattern 21:** I don't ~ as often as before. _(나는 예전만큼 자주 ~하지 않는다.)_
    
    1. I don't watch movies as often as before.
        
    2. I don't go to cafes as often as before.
        
    3. I don't go to the gym as often as before.
        
- **Pattern 22:** But I still enjoy ~ whenever I have the chance. _(하지만 나는 기회가 될 때마다 여전히 ~를 즐긴다.)_
    
    1. But I still enjoy traveling whenever I have the chance.
        
    2. But I still enjoy hiking whenever I have the chance.
        
    3. But I still enjoy watching movies whenever I have the chance.
        
- **Pattern 23:** Overall, I really enjoy ~. _(전반적으로, 나는 ~를 정말 즐긴다.)_
    
    1. Overall, I really enjoy listening to music.
        
    2. Overall, I really enjoy going to cafes.
        
    3. Overall, I really enjoy traveling.
        
- **Pattern 24:** For me, ~ is a great way to relax. _(나에게 있어서 ~는 긴장을 풀기에 아주 좋은 방법이다.)_
    
    1. For me, listening to music is a great way to relax.
        
    2. For me, going to cafes is a great way to relax.
        
    3. For me, walking in the park is a great way to relax.
